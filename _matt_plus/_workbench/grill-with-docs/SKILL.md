Read `skill://grilling` and run its interview against the plan or design in front of us.

Also read `skill://domain-modeling`. The plan or spec holds the design; the interview does not write a record per answered question. At the end, apply the ADR and glossary tests. Most grillings add zero or one ADR and at most two terms; extend an existing family or term before adding a new one.
